## 0.2.0

- New free widgets: `ElasticStepTracker`, `PullChainToggle`, `SpringTooltip` and `SpringWaffleChart`.
- New free live icons: `KUmbrellaPopIcon`, `KWifiPulseIcon`, `KRainCloudIcon` and `KDropPinIcon`.
- Premium additions (install with `npx kipluo add <name> --flutter`): Momentum Wheel Picker, Scrub Number Input, Bubble Physics Chart, Liquid Progress loader, and the Battery Charge, Winking Moon, Growing Plant and Rolling Dice icons.

## 0.1.1

- Source now public at https://github.com/AngeloGetuaban/flutter-kipluo, with repository and issue tracker links on pub.dev.
- Minimum Flutter version set to 3.38, the first release with Dart 3.10 (already required).

## 0.1.0

- First release: spring engine (`KSprings`), `KipluoTheme` styles, colours and shadow, synthesized spatial sound (`KipluoSound`).
- 36 free widgets and loaders, free bespoke animated icons and the free icon pack.
